# Version and approve formal deliverables

Daily Intelligence Briefs may publish automatically for internal use, while Weekly Operations Plans and Client Strategy Plans remain drafts until explicitly approved. Research Workpapers may evolve continuously, but every formal deliverable is versioned and an Approved Deliverable cannot be silently overwritten by later collection or model output.

## Consequences

- Exports of client-facing material require an approved version.
- Regeneration creates a new version with visible changes rather than mutating the approved record.
- Evidence and brief versions used by each deliverable must remain traceable.
