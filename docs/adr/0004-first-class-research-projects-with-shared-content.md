# Model research projects separately while sharing collected content

MediaHunter will treat each client or internal assignment as a first-class Research Project with its own Project Brief, selected evidence, interpretations, and deliverables. Accounts and articles remain shared content intelligence records that projects reference, because storing content once enables reuse while isolating project-specific relevance and conclusions prevents one client's assumptions from contaminating another project.

## Consequences

- A Research Project is distinct from a market track, owned account, monitoring program, or chat session.
- Project-to-account, project-to-article, and project-to-source relationships must preserve relevance notes and selection status.
- Analysis outputs and conclusions belong to a project even when their underlying articles are shared.
